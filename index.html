01 / About

Building toward AI, one project at a time

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at CMR University, interested in AI, software development, and building things that actually run — not just diagrams on paper. Most of what I know, I picked up by starting a project, getting stuck, and figuring it out.

That's meant desktop apps built with Tkinter, a couple of Streamlit-based ML tools, and a growing habit of showing up to hackathons with an idea and a deadline. I've also spent time on an internship applying these skills to real software and AI work, which is now part of my project history on GitHub.

Right now I'm splitting my attention between strengthening the fundamentals — data structures, databases, operating systems — and moving toward AI-focused development: exploring modern AI SDKs, the MERN stack, and what it takes to ship a full-stack AI product, not just a notebook.
